Pizza tonight

Four steps. Ninety seconds.

  1. 01

    Light it up

    Push-button gas, or kindling and a match if you're feeling traditional. 25 minutes to 900°F.

  2. 02

    Stretch the dough

    Our recipe card comes in the box. Tipo 00, 65% hydration, 24-hour cold ferment.

  3. 03

    Slide it in

    Onto a 900°F refractory floor with a kiss of flame on the rim from above.

  4. 04

    Eat it standing

    Ninety seconds later. Hot, leopard-spotted, gone in three minutes. Repeat.
