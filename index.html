04 · The Method

Six steps from
drawing to dinner.

  1. i.

    Drawn

    Each oven begins as a hand-drawn elevation pinned to a corkboard above the welder's bench. Old-fashioned, on purpose.

    Pencil · Newsprint · 2 days
  2. ii.

    Cut

    Marine-grade 304 stainless, water-jet cut to a tolerance of half a millimetre, stamped with the dome number that follows the chamber for the rest of its life.

    304 stainless · ½ mm · 1 day
  3. iii.

    Welded

    Every dome is MIG-welded by hand. Two pizzaioli-trained welders, no automation, every seam ground and finished by the hands that laid it.

    MIG · Hand-ground · 4 days
  4. iv.

    Insulated

    Four inches of ceramic-fibre blanket pack the dome, floor, and door — the same material that lines space-shuttle re-entry tiles, just slightly less stressed.

    Ceramic fibre · 4″ · 1 day
  5. v.

    Seasoned

    A twelve-hour curing fire, slow then steep, drives the moisture out of the refractory floor and tunes the dome to its final colour. The shop smells like pine and stone.

    Slow → steep · 12 hours
  6. vi.

    Shipped & lit

    Boxed flat-pack on a custom pallet, accompanied by a signed certificate, two recipe cards, and a phone number that someone in the workshop will answer.

    Pallet · Signed · 5–7 days
