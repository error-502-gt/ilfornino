Heritage

A craft we inherited by firelight.

Born in Napoli, raised by the wood-fire.

The Neapolitan oven is older than the country it lives in. We trace ours to Modena, where my grandfather lined his first dome with refractory brick and taught a stubborn kid the difference between flame and ember.

Flour, water, salt, fire — & time.

Real Neapolitan dough is alive. We design our chambers around it: a low domed ceiling that bounces 900° of heat onto the rim, a stone floor that gives the bottom a leopard-spot kiss in less than two minutes.

The atelier in Valley Cottage.

Each oven is welded by hand from heavy-gauge stainless steel, blanketed in two inches of ceramic-fiber insulation, and seasoned over a 12-hour curing fire before it leaves us. No two domes ring quite the same — that is part of the contract.

Yours for a generation.

An ilFornino is built to outlive its first cook, its first patio, its first house. We sign every chamber, ship it ready to fire, and answer the phone when you call. That is the whole philosophy.

N°04 · Anatomy of a flame

Six things only ilFornino does.

We do not buy domes from a catalogue. We do not powder-coat over the seams. Each oven is engineered, in-house, around six obsessions.

01

One Flat Cooking Surface™

A single seamless slab of medium-density refractory stone — no joints, no cold spots, no bottoms-burned-tops-raw.

02

Octagonal Double-Dome

An eight-faceted inner dome reflects flame onto the pie's rim, with a four-inch ceramic-fibre wall holding the heat in.

03

Hand MIG-welded 304 Stainless

Heavy-gauge marine-grade steel, every seam laid by a human, every seam ground and finished by hand.

04

Hybrid Wood / Gas

A propane ring tucked beside the wood-rack — push-button ignition, then drop a log on top for the flavour.

05

Twelve-hour seasoning

Every chamber is cured on a slow fire in our shop before it ships, so it is ready for service the night it arrives.

06

Lifetime workshop

If something rings wrong, we open the dome ourselves. Spare parts, second seasonings, and warranty stay in our hands.
